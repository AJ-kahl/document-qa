Project Phoenix — Internal FAQ

What is Project Phoenix?
Project Phoenix is the internal initiative to migrate our customer data
platform from the legacy on-premise servers to a cloud-based system by
Q3 2027. The goal is to cut infrastructure costs by roughly 40% and
improve system uptime to 99.9%.

Who is leading the project?
The project is led by the Platform Engineering team, with Sarah Chen as
the technical lead and Marcus Reed as the product owner. Questions should
be directed to the #phoenix channel on Slack.

What is the timeline?
Phase 1 (planning and architecture) runs January to March 2027.
Phase 2 (migration and testing) runs April to July 2027.
Phase 3 (rollout and decommissioning of old servers) completes by
September 2027.

Will there be downtime?
Minimal. The migration is designed to be incremental, with no expected
downtime during business hours. Any required maintenance will happen on
weekends and be announced one week in advance.

What happens to the old data?
All legacy data will be archived for seven years in cold storage to meet
compliance requirements, then permanently deleted.
